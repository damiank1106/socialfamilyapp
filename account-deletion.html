Account Deletion

Social Family App
Effective date: June 7, 2026
Last updated: October 28, 2026

Normal users and the Apple Reviewer can permanently delete their Social Family App account and associated data directly inside the iOS app. No email or support request is required.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to the Social Family App account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open Privacy & Data.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Complete the displayed identity and confirmation steps, then approve the final permanent-deletion warning.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Never send your password by email. The deletion flow verifies the account securely inside the app.

2. Data deleted with the account

After final confirmation, the account is removed from the active Raspberry Pi backend together with associated:

  • Profile, display name, username, password hash, role, and preferences
  • Posts, comments, reactions, and General Chat messages
  • Profile picture, cover picture, uploaded photos, and uploaded videos
  • Calendar events, descriptions, and notes
  • Notifications, notification state, APNs tokens, and device registrations
  • Access/refresh sessions and refresh-token records
  • OpenAI translation-consent choice, translations, and AI usage records
  • Synchronization records and other user-linked operational records
  • Pending/resumable upload sessions and user-linked temporary media files

The app also clears the deleted account's local sign-in session and app data from the iPhone.

3. Apple Reviewer account

The Apple Reviewer can use the same in-app deletion flow. Its fictional review-room account and associated review data are deleted without exposing or changing production family data. The review environment remains isolated from the production family environment throughout the process.

4. Backups and limited temporary retention

Account deletion removes the account and associated records from the active service and deletes active uploaded files. Historical copies may remain temporarily in administrator-controlled recovery backups until those backups are replaced or removed through normal backup rotation. Deleted data is not restored for ordinary use. Limited security records may be retained when required to protect the service or comply with law.

5. Administrator protection

Administrator accounts cannot delete themselves through this in-app flow. This protection prevents accidental removal of the production administrative account or backend infrastructure. It does not restrict a normal user or the Apple Reviewer from deleting their own account.

6. Optional help

In-app deletion does not require contacting the developer. If you cannot sign in or have a privacy question, you may optionally contact the Social Family App Developer at dedeveloper@gmail.com. Do not send your password.
