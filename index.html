Summary
Social Family App is a private, invitation-only iOS social app for approved family members. The app processes account information and the content needed to provide its social, chat, calendar, media, and notification features. Data is stored on the user's iPhone and on a privately managed Raspberry Pi backend. We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or use personal data to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

1. Scope and users

This policy applies to the Social Family App iOS application, its Raspberry Pi backend and PostgreSQL database, its administrator tools, and the separate Apple Reviewer review environment. Social Family App is not a public social network. Accounts are approved or created by the administrator and use a username and password to sign in.

Approved family users share the production family environment. The Administrator has additional moderation and service-management powers. The Apple Reviewer account is restricted to a separate fictional review environment as described below.

2. Data we process

Account, authentication, and profile data

  • Display name, username, internal user ID, role, and account status
  • A password hash; the backend is not designed to store readable passwords
  • Profile biography, profile image, and cover image when provided
  • Access and refresh tokens, session records, and login timestamps
  • Your saved OpenAI translation-consent choice

Social, communication, and calendar data

  • Posts and comments
  • General Chat messages
  • Likes and other reactions
  • Photos, videos, captions, profile media, and cover media
  • Calendar events, descriptions, dates, and related notes
  • Notification records, delivery state, and read state
  • Edits, deletion state, and synchronization history

Social posts, comments, General Chat messages, media, reactions, and shared calendar data are visible to other approved users in the same environment as part of the app's intended functionality. Production family content is not visible inside the Apple review environment.

Device, push-notification, media, and operational data

  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device tokens, token hashes, app environment, device-registration status, and notification topics
  • Media filename, file type, MIME type, size, dimensions, video duration, integrity hash, upload progress, and server media identifier
  • Dates, times, object identifiers, sync cursors, change sequence identifiers, upload sessions, and queued-operation state
  • Limited diagnostic, security, server availability, and error information needed to operate and protect the service
  • IP address and basic connection metadata necessarily processed by the server, Cloudflare, and network providers when the app connects

The app is not designed to collect precise or approximate location, address-book contacts, health or fitness information, payment or financial information, or advertising identifiers. It contains no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDK.

3. How data is used

We process data to:

  • Create, approve, authenticate, secure, and manage accounts
  • Display profiles and provide posts, comments, reactions, and General Chat
  • Upload, store, display, and synchronize photos and videos
  • Provide calendar, notification, offline, and synchronization features
  • Send requested push notifications through APNs
  • Provide AI translation only when the affected user has allowed it
  • Provide the limited Family AI social features described below
  • Prevent duplicate requests, abuse, unauthorized access, and data loss
  • Maintain backups and diagnose reliability or security problems
  • Apply administrator permissions, moderation, and room isolation

4. OpenAI / GPT-6.1 Sol translation

Social Family App can use OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol model to translate posts, comments, and General Chat text. Before any user's text is sent to OpenAI for translation, that user is shown a clear one-time choice explaining the transfer and offering Allow and Don't Allow.

  • If the user allows translation, the text being translated and the instructions needed to perform the translation may be sent from the Raspberry Pi backend to OpenAI.
  • If the user denies permission, has not yet made a choice, or later turns permission off, that user's content is not sent to OpenAI and AI translation is disabled for that user.
  • A user can review or change this choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Translation output, translation status, language information, and limited usage records may be stored with the related content so the translation can be displayed and retried when necessary.

The OpenAI API credential is held only by the private backend and is not included in the iOS app. OpenAI receives the content only to provide the requested translation service; Social Family App does not provide it for advertising or cross-app tracking. The Apple Reviewer must make the same consent choice inside the isolated review environment.

5. Remaining Family AI features and DeepSeek

Family AI Chat has been removed from the app. The backend still supports a limited Family AI social profile that can create administrator-controlled social posts and, when enabled by the Administrator, generate replies to comments placed on Family AI's own posts. These remaining generation features use DeepSeek.

When one of those features is enabled, the backend may send DeepSeek:

  • Instructions and a topic needed to generate a Family AI social post
  • The relevant comment placed on a Family AI post and limited surrounding context needed to generate a reply
  • Language, safety, and response-format instructions

Ordinary user posts, General Chat messages, passwords, authentication tokens, private media, full account records, and administrator credentials are not intended to be sent to DeepSeek. The DeepSeek API credential is stored only on the Raspberry Pi backend. There are no saved Family AI Chat conversations because that feature is no longer available.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or share it with data brokers. We do not use data to track users across other companies' apps or websites. Data is disclosed only as needed for app functionality, security, legal compliance, or the following services:

  • OpenAI: processes post, comment, or General Chat text for GPT-6.1 Sol translation only after the affected user explicitly allows it.
  • DeepSeek: processes the limited Family AI generation data described above when those administrator-controlled features are enabled.
  • Apple / APNs: processes device tokens and notification payloads to deliver push notifications to Apple devices. Notification previews may contain app activity or message information according to the user's device-notification settings.
  • Cloudflare: routes and protects encrypted HTTPS traffic between the iOS app and Raspberry Pi backend and may process IP addresses, request metadata, and security signals needed for that service.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this public Privacy Policy and the account-deletion information page. Those pages do not host Social Family App accounts or family content.

Service providers are expected to protect data consistently with their service agreements and applicable law. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, protect users, investigate abuse, or protect the app and backend.

7. Storage, synchronization, and security

On the iPhone

  • SwiftData stores synchronized app records for offline use and responsive display.
  • Keychain stores access tokens, refresh tokens, and saved sign-in/session secrets.
  • UserDefaults stores app preferences, interface state, and limited privacy-choice state used by the app.
  • App-private cache and media storage stores downloaded, pending, or temporary photos, videos, thumbnails, and related files.

These locations are protected by iOS application sandboxing and device security. Some cached or local database content is not separately encrypted by the app, so the user's passcode and iOS security remain important.

On the Raspberry Pi

Account, content, consent, notification, and synchronization records are stored in a PostgreSQL database on the privately managed Raspberry Pi. Uploaded media is stored in backend-controlled media storage, with related metadata in PostgreSQL. Encrypted HTTPS traffic reaches the backend through Cloudflare routing. Recovery backups may contain database and media copies and are kept in administrator-controlled backup storage.

Security measures include HTTPS, Cloudflare traffic protection, password hashing, hashed backend refresh-token records, encrypted APNs device-token storage, Keychain storage on iOS, role-based access controls, room scoping, upload validation, private Docker networking for PostgreSQL, and restricted backend-only storage of service API credentials. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Apple Reviewer isolation

The Apple Reviewer account operates only in a separate fictional review environment. Backend room scoping prevents that account from viewing, receiving, editing, reacting to, or otherwise accessing production family profiles, posts, comments, General Chat messages, media, calendar events, or notifications. Production users likewise do not see review-room data.

Review-room data is stored on the same privately managed backend but is assigned to a separate room and filtered on every supported operation. The reviewer account follows the same OpenAI consent rule and can delete its account and associated review-room data from within the app.

9. Retention

  • Active account, profile, post, comment, General Chat, reaction, media, and calendar data is retained while the account or content remains active, or until it is deleted using the available app controls.
  • Notification records are currently removed by backend maintenance after approximately 7 days. Device registrations and APNs tokens are retained while needed for push delivery and are removed when the account is deleted; invalid or replaced tokens may be disabled earlier.
  • Detailed synchronization-change history is periodically pruned after approximately 90 days. Short-lived upload sessions and temporary media are removed after expiration or cleanup.
  • Authentication and refresh-token records are retained until expiration, revocation, replacement, sign-out cleanup, or account deletion, as applicable.
  • Limited operational and security logs are kept only as long as reasonably needed to troubleshoot, secure, and maintain the private service.
  • Recovery backups are retained until they are replaced or removed through the backend's normal backup rotation and maintenance process. A deleted account is removed from the active service and is not restored for ordinary use from a backup. Historical backup copies may remain unavailable to users until those backups rotate out, unless preservation is legally required.

10. Account and data deletion

Normal users and the Apple Reviewer can permanently delete their account in the app without emailing support:

Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account

The app requires clear confirmation and identity verification before deletion. Once confirmed, the backend permanently removes the account from the active service together with associated:

  • Profile, username, password hash, role, settings, and consent choice
  • Posts, comments, reactions, and General Chat messages
  • Uploaded photos, videos, profile media, cover media, and media metadata
  • Calendar events and notes
  • Notifications, notification state, APNs tokens, and device registrations
  • Access/refresh sessions and refresh-token records
  • AI usage, translation, synchronization, and other user-linked records
  • Pending or resumable upload sessions and user-linked temporary files

The app clears the deleted account's local session and app data from the device. Historical copies may remain temporarily in recovery backups until normal backup rotation, as explained above. Deletion is irreversible.

Administrator accounts are protected from in-app self-deletion so an Administrator cannot accidentally remove the production administrative account or backend infrastructure. This protection does not prevent normal users or the Apple Reviewer from deleting their own accounts.

11. Your choices

  • Allow, deny, or later change OpenAI translation permission under Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Edit or delete your own supported posts, comments, and other content.
  • Control push-notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • Choose whether to provide optional profile information or media.
  • Permanently delete your account in the app as described above.
  • Contact the developer for privacy questions or assistance. Contacting the developer is not required to use in-app account deletion.

12. Children and family use

Social Family App is designed for a private family group, not for public use by unknown users. The Administrator controls account approval. Parents or guardians are responsible for deciding whether and how a child in their family may use the app and for supervising the content shared in the family environment. Users should not post sensitive information that should not be shared with other approved family members.
